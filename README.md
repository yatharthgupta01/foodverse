## 📌 Project Overview
Food Verse AI is an advanced multimodal food intelligence platform that analyzes food images, kitchen videos, and dietary profiles to recognize dishes, estimate macronutrient breakdowns, evaluate food freshness, monitor commercial kitchen safety compliance, and generate personalized 7-day meal plans.

## 🚀 Key Modules
- **Nutrition Audit (Epic 2):** Recognizes dishes and computes calories, protein, carbs, fats, fiber, allergen warnings, and composite health scores.
- **Freshness & Spoilage Diagnostic (Epic 3):** Analyzes perishable items to classify freshness (Fresh, Moderately Fresh, Spoiled) and provides preservation tips.
- **Kitchen Safety Compliance (Epic 4):** Evaluates commercial kitchens for HACCP hygiene adherence, PPE compliance, and cross-contamination hazards.
- **Smart Recipe Coach (Epic 4):** Generates zero-waste recipes based on available pantry ingredients.
- **Personalized Meal Planner (Epic 5):** Calculates BMR/TDEE targets and creates customized 7-day meal plans with automated grocery shopping lists.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend / App Framework:** Streamlit (Python 3.9+)
- **Data & Image Processing:** Pandas, NumPy, Pillow
